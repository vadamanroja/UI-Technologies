4. Understanding Padding and Margin

Padding is the space inside an element, between its content and border. Margin is the space outside the border, separating the element from others.

Outer stage

Margin example: 24px

The blue box has margin around its outside. The yellow area around it helps illustrate the external spacing.

Padding example: 28px

5. Multiple Background Images and Adjustable Sizes

This card layers a gradient and two background images. Each layer has its own size, position, and repeat setting.

Layered Background Design

The gradient is on top, the main photo fills the card, and a smaller image layer is positioned at the bottom-right.

Try changing background-size, background-position, or background-repeat in the CSS.

Example: background-size: cover, cover, 140px 140px;

The values match the order of the background layers: gradient, main image, and smaller image.
